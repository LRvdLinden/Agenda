NOTES FOR APP REVIEW (Engels, plak in "Notes")
  Weekdot is a free calendar app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. No account or login is needed.
  - On first launch the app asks for calendar access (to show and edit the user's existing calendars) and,
    optionally, notifications, reminders and location (weather forecast and travel time to events).
    Everything also works without these permissions using Weekdot's own calendars, synced via the user's private iCloud.
  - "Add calendar" > "Holidays" / "Sports" lets users subscribe to public iCalendar (ICS/webcal) feeds such as public
    holidays, school breaks, football leagues and Formula 1. These are public schedules; team and league logos
    are shown only to identify the subscribed calendar.
  - Widgets (Home Screen, Lock Screen) and Apple Watch complications show the user's upcoming events.
  - To test: tap "+" to add an event, set an alert 1 minute ahead, and tap the notification to open the event.
