Weekdot shows your whole week at a glance. A fast, clear calendar for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch — free, without accounts or ads.

YOUR WEEK, CLEARLY
• Week view with 7 days as a tidy grid of day boxes, or as a time line
• Day, multi-day, month, year and list views
• Week numbers, weekend shading, current-time line and pinch to zoom
• Drag to move or resize events, copy & paste, duplicate in one tap

YOUR OWN CALENDARS — AND THE ONES YOU ALREADY USE
• Weekdot has its own calendars, synced privately through iCloud
• Optionally show and edit every calendar from the Calendar app: iCloud, Google, Outlook, Exchange, Yahoo and CalDAV
• Give every calendar and event its own colour and emoji, or let rules do it automatically
• Templates and smart suggestions for events you plan often
• Repeating events, time zones, travel time, invitees, notes and links

SUBSCRIBE TO CALENDARS
• Public holidays for 60+ countries, school holidays and flag days
• Sports: football competitions and clubs, Formula 1, MotoGP, tennis, cycling, NFL, NBA and more
• Waste collection (Netherlands, Belgium, Norway), zodiac and astronomy
• Or add any ICS / webcal link — kept up to date automatically

ALERTS THAT TELL YOU MORE
• Notifications on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch with time, place and travel time
• Snooze, or get directions in Apple Maps straight from the alert
• Addresses fill in as you type, with a map in every event

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in the style you know: Today, Up Next, List, Month and Week — with events you can page through
• Apple Watch app for your day and week
• Watch complications: up next, today, countdown, remaining today, week number and three weeks

EVERYWHERE, IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• Native apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch

• Light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
