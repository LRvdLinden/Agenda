Let your calendar run your home. Your events of the coming days arrive on Homey, so Flows can start when an appointment begins or ends, minutes before an appointment, or with today's agenda. Filter on a calendar or a word in the title, use the tokens in Advanced Flow and add the Agenda widget to your dashboard.

Requires the free Weekdot app for iPhone, iPad or Mac. You choose which calendars are shared, and your events stay on your own Homey.

Questions and help with setting up can be found in the Homey Community topic of this app.
