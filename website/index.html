Questions

Does Weekdot replace my calendar accounts?

No. Weekdot shows and edits the calendars from the Calendar app, so everything keeps syncing through iCloud, Google, Outlook or Exchange.

Where is my data stored?

Your events stay in your own calendar accounts. Weekdot's own settings sync through your private iCloud. Weekdot has no servers that store your data.

Which languages are supported?

More than 30, including English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Arabic.

Can I add holidays?

Yes. Subscribe to the public holidays of more than 60 countries with one tap, and to school holidays and flag days, or paste any .ics link.

Which calendars can I subscribe to?

Public holidays for more than 60 countries, school holidays, flag days, sports such as football, Formula 1, MotoGP, tennis, cycling, NFL and NBA, zodiac and astronomy, and waste collection in the Netherlands, Belgium and Norway. You can also add any ICS or webcal link. Subscriptions update automatically.

Is Weekdot free?

Yes. Weekdot is free, without ads, and you don't need to create an account.

Which devices does Weekdot work on?

iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 17 or later), Mac (macOS 14 or later) and Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later). On the Mac it is a real Mac app.

Does Weekdot work with Google Calendar and Outlook?

Yes. Add your Google, Outlook, Exchange or Yahoo account to the Calendar app on your device, and Weekdot shows and edits those calendars too.

How do alerts work?

Weekdot sends notifications on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch with the time, the place and the travel time from where you are. From the notification you can snooze or open directions in Apple Maps. You choose up to two alerts per event, or a custom one.

Which widgets are there?

Up Next, List, Month, Week and Today for the Home Screen, in the style of Apple's Calendar widgets, plus Lock Screen widgets on iPhone. Use the arrows in a widget to page through more events.

What can I do on Apple Watch?

The Weekdot watch app shows your day and week. Complications for your watch face show the next event, today's date, a countdown, the events left today, the week number and three weeks at a glance.

How do I follow my favourite team or competition?

Open Calendars › Subscribe to Calendar, choose your country and tap Sports. Pick a sport, then a club or competition, and subscribe with one tap: all matches appear in your week and stay up to date.

Can I see my waste collection days?

Yes, for many municipalities in the Netherlands, Belgium and Norway. Enter your postcode and house number and the collection days are added as a calendar that updates itself.

Can I change events from calendars I can't edit?

Yes. Events in read-only calendars, such as subscriptions, are replaced by an edited copy in one of your Weekdot calendars, so the original stays intact.

Can I set the start of the week and week numbers?

Yes. Choose the first day of the week, show week numbers, shade weekends and show 1 to 7 days in the week view, as a grid of day boxes or as a time line.
