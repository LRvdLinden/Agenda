Help & Support

Sevn shows no events

Allow full calendar access: Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars › Sevn › Full Access.

How do I add a Google or Outlook calendar?

Add the account in Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts (Mac: Calendar app › Add Account). Sevn shows it automatically.

How do I change the language?

Sevn follows the language of your device. On iPhone and iPad you can choose a language for Sevn only in Settings › Apps › Sevn › Language.

Why doesn't the app icon show today's date?

Only Apple's own Calendar app can change its icon by itself. Add the Sevn ‘Today’ widget to your Home Screen for a live date. On the Mac, the Dock icon shows today's date while Sevn is open.

Do my colours and templates sync?

Yes, through iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on all your devices.
