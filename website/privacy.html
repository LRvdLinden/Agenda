Privacy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Sevn is built to keep your data yours.

Calendar data: Sevn reads and writes events only in the calendar accounts on your device (the Calendar app). Your events are never sent to us.

iCloud: Sevn's own settings, templates, calendar sets, rules, colours and emoji are stored in your private iCloud so they sync between your devices. We cannot access them.

Weather (optional): when you turn on weather, your approximate location is sent to Open-Meteo to get a forecast. Sevn does not store it.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads and no analytics.

Questions? Get in touch through the support page.
