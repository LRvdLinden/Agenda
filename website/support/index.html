Getting started

Is Weekdot free?

Yes. Weekdot is completely free, has no ads and does not ask you to create an account.

Which devices and versions does Weekdot need?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, a Mac with macOS 14 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. On the Mac, Weekdot is a real Mac app.

How do I join the beta?

Install TestFlight from the App Store, open the Weekdot invitation link on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and tap Accept, then Install. New builds appear in TestFlight, and you can turn on automatic updates there.

Join the beta
Do I need to create an account?

No. Weekdot works with the Apple Account already on your device. There is nothing to sign up for.

How do I change the language?

Weekdot follows the language of your device and speaks more than 30 languages. On iPhone and iPad you can choose a language for Weekdot only in Settings › Apps › Weekdot › Language. On the Mac, use System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications.

Calendars & accounts

Weekdot shows no events

Allow full calendar access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars › Weekdot › Full Access. Then check in Weekdot under Settings › Calendars that Show Calendar App Calendars is on, and that the calendars are switched on in the Calendars list.

How do I add a Google or Outlook calendar?

Add the account in Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts (on the Mac: Calendar app › Add Account). Weekdot shows it automatically, and you can create and edit events in it.

Some of my calendars are missing

Open Calendars in Weekdot and make sure they are switched on, and that you are not looking at a calendar set that leaves them out. Also check in Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts that Calendars is turned on for that account.

Can I use Weekdot without the Calendar app?

Yes. Weekdot has its own calendars that sync through your private iCloud, each with its own colour. You can use them on their own, or together with the calendars from the Calendar app.

How do I show birthdays and reminders?

Turn on Show Birthdays and Show Reminders in Settings › Calendars. Birthdays come from your contacts; for reminders Weekdot asks for access to Reminders.

What are calendar sets?

A set is a group of calendars, for example Work or Family. Create them under Calendars › Calendar Sets and switch between them to see only what matters right now.

Views & events

How do I change the week view?

In Settings › Views choose the start view, the week layout (a grid of day boxes or a time line), how many days you see, the first day of the week, week numbers and shaded weekends.

How do I move, copy or resize an event?

Drag an event to another time or day to move it, and drag its bottom edge to change its length. Use the event's menu to copy it to another day or duplicate it. In Settings › New Events you choose the steps an event snaps to while dragging.

What are templates and colour rules?

Templates are events you plan often, such as Gym or Dentist, that you add in one tap. Colour & Emoji Rules give events a colour or emoji automatically based on words in the title. You find both in Settings › Events.

Can I change events from calendars I can't edit?

Yes. Events in read-only calendars, such as subscriptions, are replaced by an edited copy in one of your Weekdot calendars, so the original stays intact.

How do I change or delete a repeating event?

Open the event, make your change and choose whether it applies to this event only or to this and all future events.

I hid an event. How do I get it back?

Go to Settings › Calendars › Hidden Events. There you see every event you hid and can show it again.

How do I show the weather?

Turn on Weather in Week View in Settings › Weather. Enter your town if you don't want to share your location. Forecasts come from Apple Weather.

Subscriptions & sports

Which calendars can I subscribe to?

Public holidays for more than 60 countries, school holidays, flag days, sports such as football, Formula 1, MotoGP, tennis, cycling, NFL and NBA, zodiac and astronomy, and waste collection in the Netherlands, Belgium and Norway.

How do I follow my favourite team or competition?

Open Calendars › Subscribe to Calendar, choose your country and tap Sports. Pick a sport, then a club or competition, and subscribe with one tap: every match appears in your week and stays up to date.

Can I see my waste collection days?

Yes, for many municipalities in the Netherlands, Belgium and Norway. Enter your postcode and house number and the collection days are added as a calendar that updates itself.

How do I add an ICS or webcal link?

Open Calendars › Subscribe to Calendar and paste the link. Weekdot adds it as a calendar with its own colour.

How often are subscriptions updated?

Automatically, in the background and when you open Weekdot. Changed match times or new holidays appear without you having to do anything.

Alerts & widgets

How do alerts work?

Weekdot sends notifications on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch with the time, the place and the travel time. From the notification you can snooze or open directions in Apple Maps. Choose up to two alerts per event, or a custom one.

I don't get any notifications

Check that notifications for Weekdot are allowed in Settings › Notifications › Weekdot, and that a Focus is not silencing them. Set a default alert for new events in Settings › New Events.

I get every alert twice

With Weekdot Alerts for All Calendars on, Weekdot also alerts for events from the Calendar app, and the Calendar app may alert too. Turn that setting off in Settings › Notifications, or turn off alerts in the Calendar app.

Why is there no travel time?

Travel time needs a place with an address and access to your location while you use Weekdot. Allow it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Weekdot.

Which widgets are there and how do I add them?

Up Next, List, Month, Week and Today for the Home Screen, plus Lock Screen widgets on iPhone. Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and choose Weekdot.

Why doesn't the app icon show today's date?

Only Apple's own Calendar app can change its icon by itself. Add the Weekdot Today widget to your Home Screen for a live date. On the Mac, the Dock icon shows today's date while Weekdot is open.

My widget is not up to date

Your device decides when widgets refresh. Opening Weekdot updates them straight away. Widgets show the same calendars as the app, so hidden calendars stay hidden there too.

Apple Watch & Mac

What can I do on Apple Watch?

The Weekdot watch app shows your day and week and the details of each event. Complications show the next event, today's date, a countdown, the events left today, the week number and three weeks at a glance.

How do I add a Weekdot complication?

Touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications, tap a spot and choose Weekdot. You can also do this in the Watch app on your iPhone under Face Gallery.

The watch app shows no events

Make sure Weekdot is installed on your watch and has calendar access on your iPhone. Open Weekdot on your iPhone once, keep your watch nearby and give it a moment to sync.

Is there a Mac app?

Yes, a real Mac app with the same views, sidebar and keyboard and mouse support. Your settings, templates and colours sync with your iPhone and iPad through iCloud.

Sync & privacy

Do my colours and templates sync?

Yes, through iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on all your devices.

Syncing between my devices doesn't work

Check that iCloud is on for Weekdot in Settings › [your name] › iCloud, and use Sync Now in Weekdot under Settings › Sync. Events from Google, Outlook or Exchange sync through those accounts, not through Weekdot.

Weekdot says my iCloud storage was full

When the space Weekdot may use in iCloud runs out, Weekdot removes the oldest colours and emoji of single events so syncing works again. Your events themselves are not affected.

Where is my data stored?

Weekdot's own calendars and settings sync through your private iCloud, and events in your other accounts stay there. Weekdot has no servers that store your data.

Does Weekdot track me?

No. No accounts, no tracking, no ads and no analytics. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Homey

What can Weekdot do with Homey?

Your calendar starts Flows on your Homey: when an event begins or ends, a set number of minutes before an appointment, or at a time with today's agenda. Filter on calendar or title and use the event details as tags.

What do I need?

A Homey Pro with the free Weekdot app from the Homey App Store, and your Homey account. Homey Cloud is not supported.

How do I link Weekdot to my Homey?

Install the Weekdot app on your Homey. Then open Settings › Smart Home › Homey in Weekdot, sign in with your Homey account and choose your Homey. Weekdot sends your events right away.

Homey says Weekdot is not linked

Tap Update Now in Weekdot under Settings › Smart Home › Homey and check that the right Homey is selected. Under Details you see each step of the connection. Make sure the Weekdot app on your Homey is up to date.

What does Weekdot send to Homey?

Your events of the coming 8 days from the calendars you choose, sent straight to your own Homey. With Hide Details on, only the times are sent, with Busy as the title.

How do I unlink Homey?

Tap Sign Out of Homey in Weekdot. On your Homey, open the settings of the Weekdot app and choose to unlink and delete the events.

Something not working?

These quick checks solve most problems.

  • Install the latest version of Weekdot from TestFlight.
  • Allow full access to your calendars.
  • Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on every device.
  • Check that your calendars are switched on in Weekdot.
  • Close Weekdot completely and open it again.
  • Restart your device if it still doesn't work.
